This is where I will share things that don't fit in a journal article: classroom ideas, reactions to new tools, short reads, and the occasional talk recording. Posts can be as short as a paragraph.

A post can include an image — just drop the file into the `images` folder and reference it:

![Two of my books on the shelf in my office](/images/bookshelf.jpg)

*Two of my books, on the shelf in my office at Chung-Ang University.*

And to share a YouTube video, paste its address on a line by itself and it becomes a player. Here is my doctoral supervisor Ernesto Macaro's keynote on English-medium instruction at the 2017 Oxford Educational Cloud Conference:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f41hiRCs-88

That's all it takes. The rest of this site — publications, teaching, contact details — is on the [home page](/).
